Research

Low-cost ultrasonic phased-array imaging

2025 – present · Undergraduate research

Air-coupled 40 kHz 2D imaging platform

An 8-transmitter, 1-receiver array that steers an ultrasonic beam electronically and forms a 2D image, built to find out how far careful hardware and signal processing can push very low-cost parts.

  • Designed and built the transmit and receive electronics: MOSFET-driven transmit channels and TL072-based analog receive conditioning, on separate boards with a star ground to suppress acoustic and electrical crosstalk.
  • Diagnosed a DC bias fault in the receive envelope detector and fixed it with series-capacitor isolation and new divider resistors, restoring receive dynamic range.
  • Implemented cycle-accurate transmit beamforming under FreeRTOS with full-8, left-4 and right-4 sub-aperture modes.
  • Analysed multi-channel waveform datasets for phase and amplitude consistency and characterised a systematic time-of-flight offset with a firmware-side correction.
  • Studied target localisation, grating-lobe suppression and 2D image formation under severe spatial-sampling limits.
